# Program questions

Places where `docs/program.md` is ambiguous, or where it and `PROMPT.md` pull in different directions. For each one the app takes the reading that is more conservative for the knee and keeps going (working agreement 6). Nothing here changes training content; where the data model had to bend, the model bent.

Status key: **confirmed** Nov 1 = reading taken, Shawn to confirm or overrule · **confirmed** = Shawn kept the reading · **answered** = Shawn chose; the answer follows · **open** = needs an answer · **FYI** = model decision, no answer needed. Every confirm and open item through 59 was put to Shawn on Nov 1; his answers are recorded on each line.

## Readings and progression

1. **Mileage plan is a chart image.** Read from the chart as weeks 1–12: 10, 11, 12, 9 (down), 13, 14, 15, 11 (down), 16, 17, 18, 14 (down). 2. **Step-up: height or load first?** program.md: "Step height goes up 2 in. once 3 × 10 with 20 lb dumbbells is clean." PROMPT rule 4: "height-then-load raises step height one increment before any load is added." The worked example sits at 20 lb and 6 in., which only happens if load climbs first. Reading: load climbs by double progression up to 20 lb per hand; at 20 lb, clean twice raises height 2 in. with load unchanged, repeated to the phase ceiling (8 in. in Phase 1, 16 in. from Phase 2); only then does load move again. **confirmed** Nov 1
3. **Leg extension range stop.** Phase 1 says 90° to about 45°; Phase 2 says 90° to 30°; PROMPT says the stop moves 45° then 30° before load. 5. **Stiffness past 30 minutes with nothing else flagged.** PROMPT rule 2 doesn't say. program.md's hold row is "stiffer or sorer than the day before, clears within the day." Reading: not clean; it reads hold. **confirmed** Nov 1
6. **"If the whole knee is complaining"** (next run becomes elliptical; next lift's lower-body work at Phase 1 loads). Reading: a morning back-off reading makes the next run default to an elliptical swap and gives every lower-body slot of the next lift the back-off prescription (×0.75, or the previous substitution). Engine change lands before Phase 2.
7. **Balance progression has no load** (eyes open → eyes closed → folded mat → head turns → bosu). Modeled as ordered stages under a new progressionRule `stage`: clean twice moves up a stage, back-off moves down one. **FYI**
8. **Week 4 is both a deload and Phase 2's first week**, so the new single-leg slots have no prior loads to deload from. 9. **Dumbbell cap.** When the next load would exceed dumbbellMax, the engine suggests the slot's next substitution (single-leg RDL or Smith RDL for RDLs, suitcase carry for carries) instead of a load the rack can't give. **confirmed** Nov 1
10. **Carries are in yards** (3 × 40 yd), outside the pounds/miles/seconds units. A `distanceYards` field on the prescription; progression is load only (RPE 8 or under twice → +5 lb per hand). **FYI**

## Found while building the week (M4)
