# Role Profile: Director — Presales Operations

Reference document for tailoring skills in this repo. Source: Granicus "Director - Presales Operations" job description (careers-granicus.icims.com, req 10338), captured 2026-07.

## Company Context (Granicus)

Govtech SaaS serving 5,500+ federal, state, and local government agencies across the US, UK, Australia, New Zealand, and Canada. Product areas: government communications (300M+ subscriber network), government website design, meeting and agenda management, records management, and digital services. Remote-first, distributed workforce.

## Role Summary

Leads a defined segment of the Presales organization, driving consistent execution across **Solution Consulting, Solution Architecture, and Proposal Management** to support revenue growth and improve win rates. Translates presales strategy into execution — owning how work gets done, raising the quality bar, and identifying what the function needs next to scale. Both an operator and a builder.

## Responsibilities (verbatim from JD)

### Deal engagement & sales partnership
- Partner with Sales leadership to drive pipeline progression, prioritize deals, and ensure effective technical coverage
- Engage directly on high-value or high-risk opportunities to improve win probability
- Sit in on discovery calls, solution design sessions, demos, and proposal reviews to observe execution quality and identify patterns

### Coaching & standards
- Define and reinforce what "great" looks like across presales through coaching, modeling, and feedback
- Establish and enforce standards for solution design quality, documentation, and proposal alignment

### Team leadership & development
- Lead and develop managers and/or senior individual contributors across Solution Consulting, Solution Architecture, and Proposal Management
- Support development planning and performance improvement across the team
- Identify gaps in skills, capacity, and role coverage and inform hiring profiles and workforce planning

### Operations & capacity
- Manage capacity, coverage, and resource alignment to meet pipeline demands
- Design and improve presales workflows including discovery, qualification, solution design, and handoffs

### Enablement
- Own enablement content and delivery, including training programs, onboarding, and playbooks tied to real execution gaps

### Tooling & function building
- Pilot and scale tools that improve presales execution, including AI-driven workflows and demo/proposal platforms
- Drive adoption of systems and tools that improve efficiency, quality, and consistency
- Build business cases, define operating models, and stand up new functions as needed

### Metrics
- Define and track key performance metrics such as **win rate, cycle time, and solution quality**

## Implications for Skill Tailoring

- The presales org spans three functions: **Solution Consulting (SC), Solution Architecture (SA), and Proposal Management** — skills should speak to all three, not just SEs
- The coaching lens is confirmed: observe execution, identify patterns, define "great," coach/model/give feedback
- Proposal management sits INSIDE this role's org — the `proposal-manager` skill is core, not adjacent
- Capacity/coverage management is explicit — `territory-analyzer`'s presales-load emphasis is on target
- Enablement must tie to "real execution gaps" — matches `enablement-planner`'s evidence-first design
- Development planning is explicit — `career-development` is on target; audience includes managers and senior ICs, not just early-career
- Headline metrics: win rate, cycle time, solution quality
- "Pilot and scale AI-driven workflows" — this skills library itself is part of the job
- Candidate future skills validated by JD: pursuit-review (deal review facilitation), se-capacity-planner, workflow/process designer (discovery→qualification→solution design→handoffs)
