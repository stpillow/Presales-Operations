# Enablement Planner — Cowork Prompt

```
Help me design an enablement program for my presales team.

## The Situation
Team: [Size, roles, mix of new vs. tenured]
The problem I'm seeing: [What prompted this — lost deals, weak demos, slow ramp, new product launch, manager observations]
Evidence I have: [Win/loss data, call recordings/Gong themes, funnel metrics — paste what you have, or say "gut feel only"]

## Constraints
Timeline: [When this needs to land]
Capacity: [How much selling time can this consume]
Busy seasons to avoid: [RFP season, fiscal year-end, etc.]

## What I need
1. **Diagnosis:** Is this a skill gap, a content gap, or a process gap? Be honest if my evidence is thin.
2. **Program design:** Format, components, and calendar that fit my constraints.
3. **Certification rubric** (if the skill warrants it): scenario-based, with behavioral anchors for pass/fail.
4. **Measurement plan:** Leading indicators plus deal-linked lagging metrics, with confounders stated.
5. **Manager reinforcement plan:** What managers should inspect and coach after rollout.

## Rules
- If my "gap" is actually a broken process, say so instead of designing training for it.
- Don't measure success by completion rates — tie it to deal outcomes.
- Flag it if the program eats more than 10-15% of the team's selling time.
- If I gave you no evidence, label the diagnosis a hypothesis and give me a cheap way to validate it first.
```
